For the first time, I’m training for a time, rather than just a finish. This is my daily record of the work toward BQ: the running, the food, the sleep, and how it all feels.

Starting November 2, 2026.

What is BQ? What does a 29-year-old man need to run?

BQ = Boston Qualifier: a marathon performance meeting Boston's qualifying standard for an age and gender group. At 29, I use the men's 18–34 group as a reference; the actual group is determined by age on Boston Marathon race day.

ThresholdMarathon timeMeaning
2027 BQ standard2:55:00Eligible to apply; no guaranteed place
2027 time-based acceptance2:49:432:55:00 − 5:17; latest published result

For 2027, approximately 1,000 additional places were allocated by random selection among applicants who achieved BQ but missed the time-based cutoff. The cutoff varies with demand; no time can guarantee acceptance into a future edition in advance.

Qualifier registration for 2027 has closed. The 2028 qualifying window opened September 19, 2026; its acceptance cutoff is unknown. The table is historical context, not a promise of entry for 2028. Performances must fall within the relevant window and come from a certified full marathon, using official net (chip) time. Significant net-downhill courses have additional adjustment rules. A PB alone does not establish Boston eligibility.
