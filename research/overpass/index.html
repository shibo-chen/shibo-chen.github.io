Overpass: interconnect bandwidth for diverse components

Shibo Chen | Translations:
  • Zh

Problem#

When CPUs, accelerators, and I/O share one interconnect, they compete for bandwidth. Static or centralized allocation leaves bandwidth idle in one place while another component stalls, and contention between components adds interference.

Idea#

Overpass is a flexible interconnect system with distributed resource allocation. Each router makes its own allocation decision, so bandwidth follows the traffic of each component without a central arbiter.

Result#

35%higher system performance

Overpass is the third part of my PhD dissertation, with Twine (design stage) and Zipper (bus latency).

My role#

I designed and evaluated Overpass as part of my PhD dissertation.
