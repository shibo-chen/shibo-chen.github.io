Zipper: hiding bus latency for accelerators

Shibo Chen | Translations:
  • Zh

Problem#

In heterogeneous systems, the bus between a host and its accelerators sets the pace. Past work raised bus bandwidth for data-hungry workloads. Many accelerators are latency-sensitive instead: each request waits a full round trip before the next one starts.

Idea#

Zipper is a protocol optimization layer on top of existing bus standards. It lets a device issue requests in parallel at the device and request level, and it exploits data locality. The bus round trip then overlaps with useful work.

Result#

We built the end-to-end system on a heterogeneous platform with an integrated FPGA and applied Zipper to two domains.

8×speedup for one accelerator
4.3%logic overhead for that speedup
1.5×speedup for a second accelerator, at 0.9% overhead

My role#

First author. I designed the protocol layer, built the FPGA system, and ran the evaluation.
